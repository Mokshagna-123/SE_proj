# RBI IT Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices Directions, 2023
Source ID: RBI-IT-2023
Jurisdiction: India
Version: Issued November 7, 2023; effective April 1, 2024
Source URL: https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12562

RBI IT governance directions consolidate expectations relating to IT governance, risk, controls, assurance, business continuity, disaster recovery and information systems audit for applicable regulated entities.
